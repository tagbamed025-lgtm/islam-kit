## Title
His Mother Refused to Eat for 3 Days… to Make Him Turn Back | Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas

## Sources
- Sahih Muslim 1748c. https://sunnah.com/muslim:1748c
- Sahih al-Bukhari 3728. https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3728
- Quran 29:8 and 31:15 (Saheeh International, quran.com).
- Note: the mother is named only "Umm Sa'd" in the source — no other personal name is used.

## ElevenLabs text (voiceover, no tags)

[HOOK]
A mother stops eating. And drinking.
She swears she will never speak to her son again… until he abandons his faith.
Three days pass.
This is the story of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas.

[1. THE CHOICE]
Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas accepted Islam, among the very first Muslims of Makkah. He would later say of himself that he was the first Arab to shoot an arrow for the cause of Allah.
But his mother does not accept it.
She decides to use the only weapon she has left: her son's love for her.

[2. THE FAST]
She tells him: "Allah commanded you to be good to your parents. I am commanding you now: turn back."
And she stops eating. Drinking.
One day passes. Then two. She stops speaking. She keeps her word.

[3. THE TENSION]
Sa'd loves his mother deeply. But he refuses to give in.
He answers her that even if she had a hundred souls, and they left her body one by one before his eyes, he would not abandon his religion for anything.
On the third day, weakened by hunger, she collapses.
His brother, Umarah, rushes to her and forces her to drink.

[4. THE REVELATION]
It is at this exact moment that Allah sends down verses.
The Quran commands the believer to be good to his parents, to keep good company with them in this world…
but never to obey them if it means disobeying Allah.

[5. WHAT REMAINS]
Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas will remain one of the ten companions promised Paradise, and one of Islam's greatest generals.
But before that, the first to shoot an arrow for his faith… had to stand firm against his own mother.
Between the love he held for her, and the truth he had just found.

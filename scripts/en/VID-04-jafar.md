## Title
He Made a King Weep… With a Single Speech | Ja'far ibn Abi Talib

## Sources
- Musnad Ahmad 1740 (narrated by Umm Salamah, chain graded *hasan* by al-Arna'ut). https://hadithunlocked.com/ahmad:1740
- Sirah of Ibn Hisham (context of the migration to Abyssinia).

## ElevenLabs text (voiceover, no tags)

[HOOK]
A Christian king, on his throne, begins to weep in front of a small group of refugees.
He has just heard a few verses, recited by a single man.
That man was Ja'far ibn Abi Talib. Cousin of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him.
This is the story of the speech that saved the first Muslims.

[1. THE FLIGHT]
In Makkah, the first Muslims are being persecuted. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, tells them to leave for a king known for his justice: the Negus of Abyssinia.
Among them: Ja'far ibn Abi Talib.
There, they finally find safety.

[2. THE DELEGATION]
But back in Makkah, Quraysh will not accept it.
Two men are sent to the Negus's kingdom: 'Amr ibn al-'As and 'Abdullah ibn Abi Rabi'ah, carrying gifts for the king and his bishops.
Their mission: convince the king to hand the Muslims over, without even hearing them.
They present them as foolish young men who have abandoned their people's religion.

[3. THE SPEECH]
The Negus refuses to judge without listening. He summons the Muslims and asks: "What is this religion that has separated you from your people?"
Ja'far stands up. He describes what they used to be: a people who worshipped idols, ate carrion, mistreated their neighbors, where the strong devoured the weak.
Then he describes what they have become: a messenger rose among them, known for his honesty and integrity. He commanded them to speak the truth, keep their word, treat their neighbors well, pray, and give to the poor.
This, says Ja'far, is why they fled to this land. And this king, more than any other, is the one they chose.

[4. THE RECITATION]
The Negus asks: "Do you have with you anything that came from Allah?"
Ja'far says yes. And he recites the opening of Surah Maryam.
The Negus begins to weep, until his beard is wet.
His bishops weep too, until their books are soaked.
The Negus says that this, and what Moses brought, come from the same light.
He refuses to hand the Muslims over.

[5. THE SECOND ATTEMPT]
The next day, 'Amr ibn al-'As returns, to set a trap.
He asks the Negus to question them about 'Isa, son of Mary. What the Muslims say about him could destroy them.
The Negus asks the question.
Ja'far answers, without hesitation: "He is the servant of Allah, His messenger, His spirit, and His word, placed into Mary, the pure virgin."
The Negus picks up a stick, sets it on the ground, and says this description does not stray from what he himself believes about him.

[6. THE PROTECTION]
He turns to the Muslims and declares: "You are safe in my land."
He has Quraysh's gifts returned.
The two envoys leave, humiliated, empty-handed.

[7. WHAT REMAINS]
The Muslims will stay in Abyssinia, protected, until they can rejoin the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him.
One single speech, before a king who agreed to listen… changed the fate of the first Muslims.

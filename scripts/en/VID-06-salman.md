## Title
He Searched for the Truth for 40 Years… Across 4 Countries | Salman al-Farisi

## Sources
- Musnad Ahmad, Salman's own account (vol. 5, p. 441–444) — chain discussed (Ibn Ishaq) but widely accepted by biographers, sirah-type source, same standing as the Bilal/Mus'ab narrative material.
- Sahih al-Bukhari (short narrations confirming his enslavement, manumission, and presence at the Battle of the Trench).

## ElevenLabs text (voiceover, no tags)

[HOOK]
A man spent nearly forty years of his life… searching for a single thing.
He left his country, his wealth, his freedom.
He served masters, one after another, in Persia, then in Syria.
This is the story of Salman al-Farisi, the man who searched for the truth.

[1. SON OF THE FIRE]
Salman was born in Persia, the son of a wealthy landowner.
His father keeps him locked at home, tasked with tending the sacred fire, day and night.
But one day, sent to inspect an estate, Salman passes by a church.
He hears a prayer… and something awakens in him.

[2. THE FLIGHT TO SYRIA]
He asks where this religion comes from. He is told: Syria.
His father, furious, locks him up and chains him.
But Salman learns that a caravan of Christian merchants is soon leaving for Syria.
He removes his chains… and flees with them.

[3. FROM MASTER TO MASTER]
In Syria, Salman serves a bishop. He discovers the man was secretly hoarding the poor's charity.
When he dies, Salman serves another pious man, then another, in Mosul, Nisibis, Amorium.
Each one, before dying, sends him to the next.

[4. THE LAST PROMISE]
The last monk tells him of a prophet soon to come, somewhere in Arabia.
He gives him three signs: he will migrate to a land between two fields of black volcanic rock, planted with palm trees; he will accept gifts, but never charity; and he will carry the seal of prophethood between his shoulders.

[5. ENSLAVEMENT]
On the way, merchants betray Salman. They sell him as a slave.
He passes from hand to hand, until he is brought to Madinah, to the Banu Qurayza.
And there… he recognizes the land the monk had described.

[6. RECOGNITION]
When he hears that a man in Quba claims to be a prophet, Salman brings charity.
The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, does not eat it, but distributes it.
First sign confirmed.
Salman returns with a gift. This time, the Prophet accepts it, and eats with him.
Second sign confirmed.
Then Salman looks between his shoulders… and sees the seal of prophethood.
He weeps, and embraces Islam.

[7. FREEDOM]
Salman is still a slave. To be free, he must pay three hundred palm saplings and a sum of gold.
The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, asks his companions to help him, and plants the young palms himself, with his own hands.
Salman finally becomes a free man.
He will later take part in the Battle of the Trench, an idea he himself proposed.
Forty years of searching… for a single truth found.

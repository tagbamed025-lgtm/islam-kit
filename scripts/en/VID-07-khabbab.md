## ElevenLabs text (voiceover, no tags)

[HOOK]
A man comes to complain to the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him.
He cannot take it anymore. He asks Allah to deliver them.
The answer he receives is not the one he expected.
This is the story of Khabbab ibn al-Aratt.

[1. A SLAVE WHO BECAME A BLACKSMITH]
Khabbab was born far from Makkah, in the Najd region. Captured young, he is sold in Makkah and becomes a slave, before being freed.
He learns the craft of blacksmithing, and makes swords.
He is among the very first to accept Islam. Unlike other companions, he has no wealth, no powerful clan to protect him: nothing but his faith.

[2. THE COMPLAINT]
One day, in the shade of the Ka'bah, Khabbab finds the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, lying down, his head resting on his own folded cloak.
He comes to tell him of his distress, and that of all the Muslims being persecuted.
He asks: "Will you not invoke Allah for us? Will you not pray for us?"

[3. THE ANSWER]
The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, sits up. His face has reddened.
He answers by telling of those who came before: believers seized, thrown into a trench dug for them, a saw placed on their heads to split them in two, others combed with iron combs down to the bone and sinew.
And yet… none of this turned them away from their faith.

[4. THE PROMISE]
Then he makes a promise to Khabbab:
One day, this religion will be fulfilled, to the point that a traveler could go alone from Sana'a to Hadramawt, fearing nothing but Allah, and a wolf for his sheep.
He adds: "But you are impatient."

[5. WHAT REMAINS]
Khabbab knew the hardship of Islam's early days.
But he also heard, from the Prophet's own mouth, peace and blessings be upon him, the promise of a time of peace.
A promise that patience… would see fulfilled.

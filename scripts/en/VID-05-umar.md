## Title
The Prophet Prayed for This Man to Become Muslim… and Allah Answered | Umar ibn al-Khattab

## Sources

## ElevenLabs text (voiceover, no tags)

[HOOK]
One day, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, made a supplication.
He asked Allah to honor Islam through one of two men.
The first was one of the fiercest enemies of the Muslims.
Allah answered… and the choice changed history.

[1. A FEARED ENEMY]
In Makkah, Umar ibn al-Khattab is known for his strength, and for his hatred of the first Muslims.
He is among those the companions fear the most.
To pray, some hide in distant houses, or leave the city, far from watching eyes.
No one yet imagines what Umar will become.

[2. THE SUPPLICATION]
One day, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, makes a precise supplication:
"O Allah, honor Islam through whichever of these two men is more beloved to You: Abu Jahl ibn Hisham, or Umar ibn al-Khattab."
Two powerful men. Two of Islam's fiercest opponents.
Only one will be chosen by Allah.

[3. THE ANSWER]
The narrator of this hadith, Ibn Umar, later specified one detail: of the two men, Umar was the more beloved to Allah.
And it is Umar… who takes the step.
The man who made Muslims tremble, who hunted them down in their hiding places, becomes one of them.
The Prophet's prayer, peace and blessings be upon him, has just been answered.

[4. FEAR TURNED INTO STRENGTH]
Before Umar, the Muslims lived in fear, away from Quraysh's eyes.
His companion Abdullah ibn Mas'ud would later say, in words that became famous:
"We never ceased to be strong… since Umar became Muslim."
The man they feared becomes the one who protects them. What lived in the shadows could finally come into the open.

[5. WHAT REMAINS]
Umar ibn al-Khattab will become one of the Prophet's closest companions, peace and blessings be upon him, then the second caliph of the Muslims, under whose rule Islam will see immense expansion.
But it all began with a supplication, spoken by one man alone…
and a heart that Allah chose to guide.
